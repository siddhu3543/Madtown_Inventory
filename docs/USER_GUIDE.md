# Madtown Inventory — user guide

Open the app on your phone and tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** (iPhone) or **Install app** (Android) so it opens like an app.

## Everyone
- **Find an item:** tap **Scan** and point at the QR label, or scan with your phone's camera app. Label worn? Type the number (e.g. `104`) or part of the name.
- **Item page:** shows status (Available, Checked out, Reserved, In repair, Missing, Lost, Retired), condition, owner, who has it, missing parts and history. The big button is the next sensible action.
- **Check out to yourself:** Check out → (photo, required for cameras, lenses and drones) → Check out. Due date defaults by type.
- **Check in:** open the item → Check in → tick what came back, choose condition, add a photo → Check in. If it came back worse, the app offers a repair or damage record in one tap.
- **Report a problem:** Report issue (damage, loss, theft). Found something listed as Lost/Missing? Scan it and tap **Mark as found**.
- **Extend:** on your loan, Extend due date (once for Team members; ask a Manager after that).
- **Offline:** lookups and scanning keep working from the last copy. A check-out made offline is queued and shows **NOT confirmed** until it syncs — don't hand over gear on the strength of a queued action.
- **Propose a purchase:** To-buy → Propose.

## Managers (Anand; Kittu once confirmed)
- Record loans for freelancers without a login (choose them as borrower, or "+ New borrower"), transfer loans, back-date a check-in with a reason, override an overdue block with a recorded reason, mark a loan lost.
- Add/edit items (Items → Add item), print labels (Print labels, 25 × 25 mm), correct counted stock with a reason, record missing parts and detached accessories, propose retirement.
- Repairs: open from the item, move through Reported → Sent for repair → In repair → Returned → Closed.
- Walkthrough: start (All / Location / Type / Holder), scan each item, enter counts, close, apply corrections from the variance report.
- Orders: record an order for an approved to-buy line, then **Receive and create items** — new MTMH IDs are created; add stock photos and print labels.
- Unsorted: answer the questions on the Not-yet-sorted rows and convert them to items.

## Owner (Naveen)
- Approve or reject to-buy lines, retirements, sales and write-offs.
- Settings: loan periods and photo rules per Type, conditions, reminder/escalation rules, who sees prices, users and roles, offboarding, **Sync now** for the Sheets mirror, email log.
- Audit log: every change with who, when, before and after; export CSV.
- Import review: run the sheet import (dry-run first).

## Times, dates and money
All times are IST; dates look like 16 Oct 2026; amounts in Rs. Team members never see prices.
